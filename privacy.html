Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

BizAutomations is the trading name of Tomasz Gorecki, a sole trader based in Kent, England, who is the data controller for this website. BizAutomations builds admin systems for small businesses. Our website is bizautomations.co.uk. Contact: hello@bizautomations.co.uk or 07565 345 786.

What data we collect

When you submit the contact form on our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your business name or role (if provided)
  • Your contact details (phone or email)
  • A brief description of your business admin challenges
  • The date and time of submission

We do not use cookies beyond those strictly necessary for the site to function. We do not use analytics tracking software.

Why we collect it

We collect this information solely to respond to your enquiry about our 30-Minute Admin Audit service. Your data is not used for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and it is never sold. It is shared only with the service providers listed below, who process it on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is retained for up to 12 months. If you become a client, relevant information is retained for as long as required to deliver the service and for the period required by applicable law.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@bizautomations.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

Data security

Form submissions are transmitted securely over HTTPS and processed via our automation infrastructure. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

Service providers

The contact form is processed on a server we rent from Hetzner Online in Germany. Enquiries are stored in Airtable, whose servers are in the USA, and we are notified of new enquiries through Telegram.

ICO registration

BizAutomations will register with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) before it starts trading. The registration number will be shown here.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
