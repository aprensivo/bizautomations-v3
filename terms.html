The 30-Minute Admin Audit

The Admin Audit is a 30-minute call about where the admin time in your business goes. There is no charge and no obligation. Within two working days of the call you receive a written plan, which is yours to keep whether or not you go ahead.

Build engagements

Where a client proceeds to a build engagement:

  • Scope and a fixed price are agreed in writing before work begins.
  • A 50% deposit is paid when the engagement letter is signed. Work starts when the deposit arrives.
  • The 50% balance is due once the system runs on your real data and you sign it off.
  • Each build includes 30 days of support after it goes live.
  • The running costs of your own tools and services (for example phone numbers, SMS and software plans) are paid by you.

Monthly support (retainer)

After the 30 days of support included in every build, you can choose monthly support. It is a flat monthly fee, set by the number of live workflows I run for you:

  • Essential, £79 a month: 1 live workflow, monitoring, error alerts and fixes when a connected app changes. Reply within 2 working days. Changes at £90 an hour.
  • Standard, £169 a month: up to 5 live workflows, everything in Essential, up to 1 hour of small changes a month and a one-line monthly report. Reply within 1 working day. Extra hours at £70 an hour.
  • Priority, £299 a month: up to 10 live workflows, everything in Standard, up to 3 hours of small changes a month and a 30-minute quarterly review. Reply the same evening on working days. Extra hours at £60 an hour. Places are limited.
  • Custom: more than 10 workflows, or new builds every month, quoted separately.

For every level:

  • A workflow is one process you can name, such as "enquiry to quote" or "missed-call text-back". Sub-workflows and error handlers do not count separately.
  • Extra hours are billed in 30-minute blocks. Work bigger than a small change is quoted as a small build.
  • Unused hours do not roll over to the next month.
  • Support is rolling monthly with no lock-in. You can change level or stop at any month end.
  • Your tools stay on your own accounts, and you pay their running costs directly.
  • Founding rate: the first 5 clients to sign up for monthly support keep these prices. Their prices are reviewed once a year, and any rise is capped at UK inflation (CPI).
  • BizAutomations is not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.
